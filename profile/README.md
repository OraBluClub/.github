## What we do

Upload a PDF, Word, or Final Draft script. Storiara breaks it into scenes, cast, locations, props, and wardrobe, then builds your schedule, budget, and call sheets from that breakdown. No retyping scripts into spreadsheets.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🎬 **Script breakdown** | Scenes with page counts in eighths, plus characters, locations, props, wardrobe, and music & effects |
| 📅 **Scheduling** | Stripboard, auto-filled Day Out of Days |
| 💰 **Budgeting** | Top sheet built from your cast, crew, locations, and gear. Exports to Excel |
| 📋 **Call sheets** | Generated per shoot day, emailed or shared by link |

Built for film schools, commercials and documentaries, and feature productions.
